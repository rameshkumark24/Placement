SECTION A: CHART RULES (The Engine)
─────────────────────────────────────────────

RULE 1 — 15-Minute Close Confirmation
Trade ONLY if a 15-minute candle CLOSES clearly beyond support
or resistance. Wick rejection near the level = sellers/buyers
present → NO TRADE. Never enter based on live price or partial
candle. Rule 31 (Green Spark) is the only exception — see Rule 31
for exact conditions.

RULE 2 — Holiday-Eve Time Restriction
If next day is a market holiday:
→ Trade ONLY between 9:15 AM – 10:30 AM.
→ NO trades after 11:00 AM (sideways action + theta decay).

RULE 5 — Chart Structure (The Engine)
Timeframe: 15-Minute Chart ONLY.
Identify: Lower highs / Higher lows / Clear rejection candles /
Well-defined support (floor) and resistance (ceiling).
No clear structure → NO TRADE.

RULE 6 — Rejection Candle Respect
If price attempts a breakout and gets rejected → treat it as a
warning. Rejection = active sellers/buyers defending the level.
Do not fight it.

RULE 8 — Breakout/Breakdown Needs Candle Confirmation
Enter ONLY after a confirmed candle close beyond the level.
Never enter on anticipation. No close = No entry.

RULE 9 — Follow-Through Speed Rule (UPDATED)
After any breakout/breakdown:
→ Nifty MUST move minimum 15 points within 10 minutes.
→ If less than 15 points in 10 minutes → EXIT immediately.
→ Stalling = setup failure. Do not wait for SL to hit.

RULE 11 — False Breakout / Trap Detection
If level breaks but next candle stalls OR turns opposite color:
→ Treat as false move.
→ EXIT IMMEDIATELY. Do not wait for Stop Loss to hit.

RULE 3 — The OI Trinity (MERGED: Old Rules 3 + 7 + 16)
Three laws in one:
3a. Heavy OI writers = The Wall. NEVER trade INTO a wall.
    Entry is allowed ONLY after OI unwinding confirms.

RULE 23 — Sandwich Trap

market. Volatility dies. Theta eats premium.
→ NO TRADE. Wait for one side to collapse first.

from overnight positions being squared off, not new trends.

Check ATM IV (Implied Volatility) shown in Option Chain before entry.

IV 20–25 → Expensive. Reduce confidence. Consider skipping.
IV > 25 → DANGER ZONE. Premium will crush on any sideways move.
           → NO TRADE regardless of chart setup.

Why: High IV = You overpay for options. Even if direction is right,
IV crush after event kills your premium. Always check before entry.
